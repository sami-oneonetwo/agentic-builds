# studio-c · SETTLEMENT art direction (motion + readability first)

1. **Silhouette language: "lanterns".** Every creature is a tapered, bottom-heavy body seen from the front in 3/4 top-down: one of four profiles (pebble, kite, bell, pear), two big ink button-eyes with a single highlight on a cream face patch (goggle / bib / blaze / face), a crest on the crown (sprout / horns / fan / curl / tuft), a tail peeking from one flank (tuft / ribbon / fan), two stub feet, blush. No arms, no round blob, no yellow default. All eight traits come from sha1(username); nothing is hand-picked.
2. **Colour from the name, never from the grass.** The 31-multiplier name hash picks a hue on a 270° wheel that skips lime-to-teal (70-160°), so a creature is always a lit dot against the meadow, even at 320x180. Two colours per creature (main + accent at +150°) plus cream and ink; the outline is the main hue darkened toward ink.
3. **Sticker outlines.** Every part carries its own 1-2 px dark outline (drawn at 4x, downsampled premultiplied). Overlaps read as lines, so a 40 px sprite survives a 3 Mbps encode and the 4x downscale to the thumb.
4. **Designed for how it moves.** Ten frames per creature: idle0/idle1 (breathing squash 6 %), walk0/walk1 (±5° lean, alternating feet), hop (stretch 0.9x1.12, raised 0.3 body heights, shadow shrinks), blink, speak (open mouth), sleep (wider, lower, crest droops, feet tucked), emote0 joy (squint arcs, grin, sparkles), emote1 love (big highlights, heart). Squash-and-stretch happens in geometry, not by scaling the bitmap.
5. **Growth is visible.** Tiers: seedling 26 px (crest bud), hatchling 32, settler 40, elder 48 with a full crest and an accent band. Same silhouette, more presence.
6. **Blink cadence, hop arcs and breathing are the compositor's job**, the sheet just supplies the poses: idle0↔idle1 every ~0.8 s, blink 1 in 6 idles, hop = 4 ticks of the hop frame on a parabolic offset, sleep frame alternates with idle1-sized breathing.
7. **Tiles are flat and chunky.** 32 px tiles: one base colour and a few bold marks (grass tufts, ripple crests, pebbles, furrows, cobbles). Nothing crosses a tile edge; organic biome edges (coast line, path edges) are drawn by the renderer from the per-pixel category map as a 2 px darkened line, with foam on the water side.
8. **Nature moves in phases, not per frame.** Wind (4 phases, played 0 1 2 3 2 1) leans grass and flowers and sways canopies and banners; ripple (4 phases) drifts water crests and dithers the foam. The 16 wind×ripple backgrounds are baked once (~110 ms each) and the frame just picks one, so the whole world animates for free.
9. **Seasons recolour, growth restages.** `season` is a float 0-4 (spring, summer, autumn, winter) that recolours grass, hills and trees; farms have 4 growth stages; trees come in 3 sizes.
10. **Buildings say whose they are.** Roof = builder's main colour, trim = accent, walls cream plaster, wood is wood. Roof type, chimney, door position, windows and the roof mark come from the username hash; the front wall strip and lit windows at dusk give the 3/4 read.
11. **Light is warm and honest.** Evening: warm multiply plus additive lantern and window glows. Dawn: cool wash, low mist over low ground, a soft sun from the top-right. Cloud shadows drift with the wind.
12. **Text stays off the world.** 64 px header (title 40 px, 22 px sides), 192 px bottom band with three rounded translucent panels (22 px body, 22 px display titles), 20 px caption strip. On the world only show-on-speak name pills (20 px bold, a dot in the creature colour). Everything text is >= 20 px.
13. **Fonts:** Arial Rounded Bold for display (middle dots are drawn, the font has none), Verdana / Verdana Bold for body and pills.
14. **What makes them ours:** the tapered lantern silhouette family, the crest+tail+patch trait system, the grass-skipping hue wheel, sticker outlines on every part, and the rule that every named creature is a real chatter and nature (not NPCs) supplies the life.
15. **Budget (measured, M-series Mac, numpy):** 20 creatures + 3 cloud shadows + 3 pills + smoke on 1280x440 composes in ~2.5 ms mean, 2.7 ms p95 (bench in render_mockup.py); a background bake is ~110 ms and happens off the frame clock; a creature's 40-frame sheet renders in ~1.3 s at hatch (34 ms/frame), cached forever.
