# studio-b — SETTLEMENT art direction (top-down, "lead with the world")

1. **Silhouette language: things that grow.** Every pip is a compact glossy pebble body (pebble / drop / bean / pear) with a SPROUT on its crown — leaf, twin-leaf, fern curl, grass tuft, bud or twig — plus optional side motifs (nubs, fins, droop flaps), a back tuft or curl, two peeking feet and big glazed eyes set low on the face. The sprout is the family signature: it grows another leaf/bud at each growth tier, so an elder reads as an elder from its crown alone.
2. **Everything from the name.** sha256(username) fixes hue (24 stops), saturation/lightness, body shape, sprout, ears, tail, pattern (plain / belly / freckles / saddle / stripes), eye size and gap, blush and the side the sprout leans. Same name → same pip, forever. A builder's cottage roof is the same colour as their pip.
3. **Ours, not theirs.** No yellow round blobs, no franchise shapes: muted glossy hues (sat 0.42–0.68), a plant crown instead of hair/ears, feet that peek from under the body, cream-and-ink eyes tinted with the complementary hue, and a dark *coloured* outline (never black).
4. **Glossy toy shading, one light.** Light from the top-left: a soft gradient, one specular blob, a faint rim light bottom-right, a blurred contact shadow. The same recipe shades roofs and the well, so creatures and buildings feel made of the same material.
5. **Animation is squash-and-stretch on a 10-frame sheet:** idle0/idle1 (breathe + glance), walk0/walk1 (lean + alternating feet + sprout sway), hop (stretch, feet tucked, shadow detaches), blink, speak (talking mouth + chirp arcs), sleep (squashed, drooped sprout, rising bubbles), emote0 joy (arc eyes, grin, sparkles), emote1 wow (wide eyes, "o", floating rings). Emotion is read from eyes + mouth + sprout, never from text.
6. **Growth tiers:** sprig 36 px → pip 48 → grown 58 → elder 70; the body scales, the sprout gains parts. At 320x180 a pip is still a lit coloured dot with a dark rim.
7. **The land is the hero.** 32 px tiles, painted at 4x and downsampled, textures wrap toroidally so there are no seams. Grass has 4 kinds by moisture and a 4-phase wind wave (blades lean, brightness passes); water has 3 variants x 4 ripple phases with drifting arcs and blinking glints; foam edges are a 16-mask x 2-phase set on the water side of every shore; trails are 16-mask connected bands at 3 wear levels; farms go bare → sprout → ripe; hills get a lit lip and a dark south slope.
8. **Palette:** grass #74B04F family (meadow #86BC58, lush #5FA34A, dry #9DB85A), forest floor #3E7A3A, canopy #3E8E45/#7BC262, deep water #2E6FB0, shallow #4E9AD0, foam #EAF6FF, sand #E3CF98, dirt #B08A58, crop gold #E5B84A. Time of day is a single multiply baked into the atlas and sprites (evening 1.10/0.90/0.72, dawn 0.93/0.98/1.06); pips keep more of their own colour so they pop.
9. **Cottages are composed, not drawn:** wall (plaster / stone / timber / whitewash-with-beams) + gable / hip / round roof in the builder's colour + chimney, arched door, cross windows, and one of planter / fence / banner / lantern. Three stages (hut, cottage, house with annex). At night windows and lanterns glow and chimneys smoke.
10. **Nature is alive, people are honest.** Wind, ripples, foam, cloud shadows, tree sway, smoke and glints move every frame regardless of who is online; every named creature and every building belongs to a real chatter; there are no animals or NPCs.
11. **HUD stays off the world.** 72 px header (title, 44 px counter with a drawn honey dot, LIVE, next-event bar) and 108 px footer (COLONY / KEEPER / CHAT LOG / minimap). On the world: only speech bubbles of pips speaking right now and their 20 px name tags; nothing smaller than 20 px anywhere. Dark pine panels (86 %), 2 px cream edge, honey accent, Arial Rounded Bold + Avenir Next.
12. **Budget:** the frame is one atlas gather + a per-tile cache of terrain-with-props (built lazily, ~6 MB) + ~20 pip blits: 3.2 ms avg / 3.5 ms p95 on this Mac for 1280x532 with 20 pips, 389 propped tiles, wind/ripple/foam/cloud phases (first frame 12 ms while the cache warms).
