# SETTLEMENT art style — studio-a ("Sprouts")

1. **Silhouette language.** Every creature is a soft PEAR-POD (wide hips, rounder head, 3/4 top-down) crowned by a SPROUT: seedling stub at tier 0, a leaf/curl/twin/bell at tier 1, a five-petal bloom at tier 2. The sprout is the growth meter and the mood meter: it perks up when they speak or hop, droops when they sleep, and bends toward whoever they talk to. Name-family ears (none / nubs / petal-ears) and a secondary-colour tail tuft (tuft / curl / fan) sit behind. No yellow round blobs, no animal faces.
2. **Everything is the name.** `sha1(username)` gives hue, saturation, a secondary hue 36-88 degrees away, head shape (round/tall/wide/crown), sprout, ears, tail, cheeks (blush/freckles/plain), eye size and belly patch. The same hash colours their hut roof and banner, so a hut and its owner match at a glance. Deterministic: the same name always hatches the same creature.
3. **Faces that read at 40 px.** Big cream eyes with a plum pupil, two glints and an upper eyelid that cuts into the eye (the lid is what makes them look alive rather than staring). Closed eyes are a "u" arc, happy eyes a "^" arc, curious eyes are wide. Cheeks and a small mouth; tiers are 32/40/48 px boxes and at 320x180 they survive as a lit dot in their colour plus a soft glow.
4. **Animation principles.** Squash-and-stretch on the foot pivot (hop: 0.86 x 1.18 and lift, idle breath 1.04 x 0.965), head tilt toward the listener on `speak`, blink and sleep as arcs, marks as shapes not glyphs (zz bubbles, sparkles, a bang). Ten frames: idle x2, walk x2, hop, blink, speak, sleep, emote x2; facing is a flip. All frames pre-rendered per name into `creatures.SpriteCache`.
5. **Rendering.** Supersampled (4x) signed-distance shapes composited premultiplied, then box-filtered, so edges are soft and rounded with a dark-of-the-same-hue outline, never black. Lit from the upper-left with a rim shade at the bottom of the pod. Same painter for buildings.
6. **Palette.** Warm dusk. Land grades toward orange at evening (x1.06, 0.82, 0.62) while water keeps its own blue grade; dawn is cool with a mist bank toward the sea. HUD is warm plum (58,40,62) with a gold hairline (222,178,96), cream ink (250,242,228). Creature hues span the whole wheel so a crowd is a bouquet.
7. **Tiles.** 32 px atlas from periodic noise (integer wave numbers) so every tile wraps seamlessly and every animated family loops in 4 phases: grass tufts lean with the wind, water crests travel, meadows carry flowers, farms grow through 4 stages, trails wear in two levels. Terrain is splatted from the atlas with smooth per-pixel masks (foam line, wet sand, depth shading, hill lip); a strict autotile path (16-mask shore/sand/hill/forest edges) is also generated.
8. **Nature is alive; people are honest.** Wind, ripples, cloud shadow, lantern flicker and sway are always on, even with nobody there. Every named creature and every hut is a real chatter; the empty world says so in the panels, not with fake life.
9. **Buildings.** A kit of 15 parts (walls, cone/hip/dome roofs, door, window, chimney, banner, fence, garden bed, lantern, well, sign, cairn). Roof = builder primary, trim = builder secondary, wall material by hash. Tier 0 is a tent and a stake, tier 2 adds a chimney and a banner. Domes wear a secondary-colour eave ring so a green dome never reads as a tree.
10. **HUD.** Text lives in the 72 px header and the 208 px footer; the 1280x440 world carries only creature speech plates and vote markers. Nothing under 20 px. Arial Rounded for headings and the one big number, Avenir Next for body, names always in their creature colour.
11. **What makes them ours.** The sprout-as-growth motif, the pear-pod stance, name-derived secondary colours on tail/ears/roof, arcs for closed eyes, and a village that is literally a bouquet of chatters' colours. Nothing here is a yellow blob, a franchise creature or an AoE villager.
12. **Budget.** World composite at 20 creatures with glows, 3 speech plates, lantern flicker and one cloud-relight slice: ~3.0 ms avg, 3.8 ms max on this Mac (numpy, Python 3.9). Terrain and buildings are baked per camera move (~0.6 s for 4 phases); relighting is spread 1/8 of the region per frame.
