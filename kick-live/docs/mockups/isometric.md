# isometric · SETTLEMENT concept render

Files: `isometric_A_dawn_empty.png`, `isometric_B_busy.png`, `isometric_thumb_320x180.png`; regenerate with `isometric_render.py` (numpy + pillow, every asset procedural, pips from `stream/world/pips.py`).

- **Camera.** 2:1 iso tiles (48x24 px, 12 px per elevation step) on a 47x61 tile window of a larger seed-generated map; the back row of tiles forms the horizon ridge, with fogged haze hills, sky, sun/moon and clouds above it. The camera is a slow pan that follows the centre of mass of awake creatures, clamped so the village stays on screen; it never cuts, never zooms. HUD: header + countdown stay as the thumbnail hook, and the colony / keeper / chat_log strip stays but goes translucent (world runs behind it) — the prison feel came from opaque boxes, not from the strip's information, and the chat log has to exist for moderation on the VOD.
- **Terrain.** seed → value-noise elevation (3 octaves) + moisture → stepped levels 0-5 → biomes (river, bank, grass, meadow, forest, dry hill, rock). The river is carved as a valley whose bed steps down front-ward (two falls), so it visibly flows; the footbridge is where the trail crosses it. Fully procedural: a new seed is a new valley, but the seed is stored in world.json so the *same* valley is there tomorrow.
- **At zero viewers** nothing named exists on screen, but the world lives: grass bends with the wind (per-tile phase), river ripples drift, clouds cross and cast shadows, the wheel turns, mist sits in the low ground at dawn, the sun/moon track the real clock. Sleepers rest at their own hearth (inside their hut) or, if they have no plot yet, curled by the trail with a dimmed label — Frame A shows exactly one such sleeper, honestly counted as `0 AWAKE`.
- **"Your own space" at 1000 chatters.** A plot is one tile claimed by `build` (hut) or `plant` (field), always adjacent to an existing plot or the trail, so the village grows outward as a contiguous settlement; the map is far larger than the camera window, so 1000 plots are a town with streets, not a crush. Plot marker post + roof/crop colour = the owner's hashed name colour; the owner's name is drawn on the plot on a 5 s rotation (and always when they speak). Above 40 awake, creature labels switch to label-on-speak; above 200 awake the camera widens one step and the strip shows `+N more out of frame`. Plots never decay while the stream is off and cannot be built over; only their owner (or a keeper build the collective voted for) changes them.
- **Honesty rules kept.** Every named creature and every plot is a real chatter; no animals, no NPC villagers; the keeper is a lantern on the trail (not drawn here), never a figure. All text is at screen scale, ≥ 20 px (56 px header count, 24/22/20 px body).
