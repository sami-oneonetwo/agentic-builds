# sidescroll — SETTLEMENT concept (side view, parallax, one very long world)

Concept render, not the product; names are examples. Frames: `sidescroll_A_dawn_empty.png` (day 1, one sleeper under a tree, no bridge yet, a natural ford), `sidescroll_B_busy.png` (night 4, 14 settled / 9 awake, huts, gardens, chat-raised bridge, festival fire), `sidescroll_thumb_320x180.png`. Regenerate with `sidescroll_render.py` (numpy + Pillow; creatures come from `stream/world/pips.py` at 6 px per sim px, up from the cave's 4, because an open frame needs bigger bodies).

**Layout call: the world takes the full frame with a translucent HUD.** A side view lives on its horizon; an opaque 144 px strip would push the ground line into the middle and kill the "space" the owner asked for. So: header 0–72 keeps the hook (56 px counts, NEXT EVENT, LIVE + watching) as a 65 % band over the sky, the countdown bar stays at 66–72, a thin **position bar** at y 79–85 shows the camera's window along the world with one coloured dot per settled plot, and colony / keeper / chat_log shrink to two truncated lines each in a 58 px translucent strip (634–692) above a 28 px caption bar. Every text item is ≥ 20 px (Menlo 20/22/24, HN Medium 22, Arial Black 40/56).

**Camera rules.** The world is a strip (seed → 1-D elevation + moisture → shore, meadow, forest, hills, and a river that descends from the far hills into a near crossing). Parallax: far hills 0.10–0.22×, forest 0.35×, plain 0.5×, ground 1×. The camera never cuts: it follows the newest speaker with a capped pan (≤ 60 px/s, eased) and shows their name on the position-bar label ("following @kaifrost"); after 20 s with no one new it drifts east at 12 px/s to the world's end, then west. A speaker more than three screens away triggers a 1 s dusk-fade "travel" rather than a long pan. Rooftops, the bridge deck and the ground line are the three walkable rows; labels stagger into a second row on collision and switch to label-on-speak above 40 awake (WORLD.md §5 density rule).

**At zero viewers** nothing about *people* is faked, but nature runs: sun and moon on the real clock (dawn gold → noon blue → sunset amber → starry night), clouds drift and shade with the sun, grass and crops bend to a wind field that gusts, the river ripples and the ford stones stay wet, smoke rises only from a hut whose owner is asleep inside, lanterns swing after dusk. The header reads `1 SETTLED · NOBODY AWAKE`, the plank says `say anything in chat. a creature hatches with your name.`, and the sleeper's line says who fed them (or "fed by nobody yet").

**"Your own space" at 1000 chatters.** Each real chatter owns one **plot**: a 240 px stretch of ground whose world-x is hashed from their username (deterministic, so it is the same place every night), so the world simply grows by 240 px per settler (1000 settlers ≈ 240 000 px ≈ 190 screens; the position bar becomes a density map of dots). Your creature roams anywhere, but your marks (`build`, `plant`, `paint`, `fence`) land only on your plot; neighbours help by asking (`help @name`), and a hut appears on a plot after 5 own marks or 3 helpers. Where plots cluster (hash collisions become neighbours), the keeper raises shared works between them from chat's asks (bridge, mill, fire): those carry the askers' names ("raised by @oskr @Mxrbles @tomo_99"). Nothing decays offline, nothing can be griefed (marks are positive-only and confined), and the camera visits the busiest 5 % of the strip most, so a village forms where people actually are rather than where a designer put it.
