# topdown — SETTLEMENT concept render (3/4 top-down, region of a 256x256-tile map)

Concept render only; names are examples. Generated by `topdown_render.py` (numpy + pillow, seed 4471): value-noise elevation and moisture decide sea, shore, grassland, meadow, forest and hills; huts are composed from each builder's name hash (base, roof, door side, chimney, pennant) in their name colour; creatures are the live `stream/world/pips.py` sprites at 4x. The river in the render is a designed meander carved into the terrain so the frame composes; the product traces rivers downhill from the hills to the sea (same carve, real path).

**Camera.** The world takes the full frame under the header; the colony / keeper / chat_log strip stays, but as a translucent HUD (y 600-696) so the land runs under it and the frame never reads as boxed-in. The camera frames 40x27 tiles of a 256x256 map and follows life: it centres on the settlement's plaza (the centroid of huts, 13 rows down so the ring clears the plank), pans to a bubble or an event when one lands, and never cuts (glides at most 1 tile/s). The minimap (top right) is honest: only walked or seen tiles are painted, the rest stays parchment, with the real explored percentage under it.

**Zero viewers.** Nature runs regardless of people: wind bends the grass tufts along a slow noise field, the river ripples and the shore foams, cloud shadows drift across the land, the sun/moon dial follows the real clock and the light grades from dawn gold through noon to evening amber. Sleeping creatures lie beside their own huts with a dim `@name · asleep` label; chimneys only smoke when the owner is awake. Nothing else moves: no animals, no NPCs, no invented villagers. The 56 px header count and the countdown stay the thumbnail hook (`2 SETTLED · 0 AWAKE` is still a legible tile).

**"Your own space" at 1000 chatters.** Each real chatter owns one plot, granted on their first message: a hut site on the ring nearest their hashed angle, expanding outward ring by ring (9 huts per ring at radius 6.6, 8.2, 9.8 ... tiles), so a thousand huts form concentric rings and worn trails that the camera can pan across; the map holds ~65k tiles, so 1000 plots use under 5%. Building is a verb (`build`, `plant`, `dig`, `fence`) that only edits your own plot; the shared plaza, monument, stones and trails are raised by votes and by keeper builds, never by one chatter. Labels switch to label-on-speak above 40 awake; huts keep their pennant colour so a plot stays identifiable without text.
